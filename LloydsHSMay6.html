Executive Overview

The week of 11–16 May 2026 is one of the most consequential in the near-term calendar, combining a dense schedule of Lloyds Banking Group activity — including the Annual General Meeting in Edinburgh, the Retirement Report publication, the Stripe partnership launch, and the Financial Empowerment campaign — with a politically charged external environment dominated by the King's Speech and State Opening of Parliament on Wednesday.
