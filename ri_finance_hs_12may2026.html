Executive Overview

The week of 11–17 May 2026 is a moderate-intensity period for UK financial services intelligence, anchored by the confirmed AFME/PRA Securitisation Roundtable on 14 May and the active PRA Dynamic General Insurance Stress Test running throughout May. The FCA's new safeguarding rules for payments and e-money institutions come into effect this month, while the proximity of the King's Speech on 13 May makes this a pre-legislative watch period for financial services. Analysts should verify ONS CPI and Labour Market release dates directly before the week begins — a potential mid-week resource pressure point if they coincide with the AFME/PRA roundtable.
